Exhibition Feature · Sienna Creative House

Art Exhibition
Unfiltered · Unbiased · Accessible

Sienna Creative House's debut exhibition brings together 61 visual artists and creatives from across Pakistan, creating a space where independent practices, different materials and distinct ways of seeing can exist side by side.

12th September – 19th September
61 artists & creatives
House 79A, Street 61, F-11/4, Islamabad
Sienna Creative House

The Exhibition

Many voices, one shared space.

Sienna Creative House presents its debut exhibition as a meeting point for independent artistic voices from across Pakistan. Rather than building the exhibition around a single subject or prescribed aesthetic, the show is structured around the idea of polyphony: multiple artistic voices retaining their individual character while contributing to a larger, shared cultural conversation.

The catalogue describes the exhibition as deliberately unfiltered, unbiased and accessible, with a merit-based selection intended to move beyond traditional gallery gatekeeping. Artists from different cities, backgrounds and stages of practice come together within one platform, allowing contemporary art to be encountered without a requirement that every work conform to one fixed theme.

Across the exhibition, the practices touch on folklore, archival memory and material traces, psychological and domestic spaces, cultural geography, classical heritage and hybrid media. The range is part of the curatorial idea itself: difference is not narrowed into one argument, but allowed to remain visible.

The exhibition's premise is simple: let different practices speak in their own language, and let the audience discover the connections.

In that sense, Sienna's debut is as much about creating a platform as presenting a group of works. It places accessibility alongside artistic plurality and gives emerging and independent practices a shared public context in Islamabad.

The Team

Shaping the platform.

Curator

Zaid

A visual artist, educator and curator based in Islamabad, Zaid's academic background includes the National College of Arts and Beaconhouse National University. His role at Sienna supports the exhibition's plural structure and its focus on independent practices.

Director

Seher Chohan

An interdisciplinary artist and creative director working across technology and visual art, Seher Chohan leads Sienna Creative House and its commitment to creating a more accessible platform for artists and creative practices.

Creative Director

Alizay Nadeem

An Islamabad-based multidisciplinary artist and creative director, Alizay Nadeem works across visual communication, contemporary art, exhibition design and creative strategy, shaping Sienna's wider visual direction.

Designer

Arbab Zaidi

A Lahore-based designer and marketing strategist, Arbab Zaidi works across visual identity and creative campaigns, translating the breadth of Sienna's exhibition programme into a cohesive public-facing design language.
