Untold
Stories

A collective exhibition bringing together 89 artists and a wide range of emerging contemporary practices, personal narratives and visual languages from across Pakistan.

Venue · DOT Gulberg, Lahore On display · Until 30 August 2026 Curated by · Mahaa Malik & Maham Malik

Exhibition Feature

89 Artists. One Collective Story.

Untold Stories creates space for artists to be seen, documented and encountered beyond the conventional boundaries of a gallery.

Presented by Cosmopolitan Art Magazine in collaboration with DOT, the exhibition brings together painting, printmaking, mixed media, works on paper, collage, installation and experimental practices. Rather than asking artists to speak in one visual language, the exhibition celebrates the differences between their approaches, materials and experiences.

At the centre of Untold Stories is a simple idea: every artist carries a story worth making visible. At DOT Gulberg, visitors can encounter these works in an open, everyday setting where art becomes part of conversation, community and discovery. The exhibition remains on display through 30 August 2026.

Selected Highlights

Adan Fatima works through oil painting and observation; Aiman Zulfiqar explores the expressive possibilities of linocut and reduction printmaking; Aliya Umair translates colour and geometric structure into jewel-like painted surfaces; and Arooj Sultan places gesture and psychological expression at the centre of portraiture. Material experimentation continues through Ayesha Rahman Hamid, while Brishna A.K works with gouache on wasli. Saira Dar uses paper collage as a language of fragmentation and composition, while Sehar Seerat Naqvi and Zahra Ishaq bring distinct approaches to oil painting and sepia-based works on paper. These highlights are only a glimpse into the wider range of practices represented throughout the exhibition.
