Exhibition Feature · Cosmopolitan Art Magazine × DOT

Unspoken —
Stories Left Untold

89 artistic voices from across Pakistan15–30 August 2026DOT Gulberg, LahoreCurated by Mahaa Malik & Maham Malik

Unspoken invited emerging artists to share stories they had never had the chance to tell—memories, transformations, joy, heartbreak and experiences that shaped who they are.

Through painting, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, installation and works on paper, the exhibition brought private experiences into public conversation. Presented by Cosmopolitan Art Magazine in collaboration with DOT, it welcomed 89 artistic voices and remained on display at DOT Gulberg, Lahore until 30 August 2026.
