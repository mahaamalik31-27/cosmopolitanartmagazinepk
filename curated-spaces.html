A thoughtfully curated space is more than a setting for objects. It is an invitation to pause, to notice texture, to recognise a story, and to imagine art as part of everyday life. At Sympl Living in DHA, Lahore, Cosmopolitan Art Magazine brought contemporary artworks into conversation with interiors and furniture, allowing the visual language of painting, collage and material exploration to become part of the space.

How We Curated the Space

Our approach begins with the artwork. Rather than treating paintings as finishing touches, we consider how each piece can create a dialogue with the surrounding environment. Scale, palette, texture and sightlines all shape the way visitors encounter a work.

For the Sympl Living presentation, the curatorial emphasis was on contrast and balance: expressive surfaces against considered furniture, intimate works alongside architectural space, and moments of colour that invite visitors to look closer. The result is a conversation between contemporary artistic practice and the experience of living with art.

“Art should not simply occupy a wall. It should change how a space is experienced.”

Working across multiple media, our selected artists bring different perspectives to the idea of a lived environment. Through this collaboration, we extend their visibility beyond the traditional gallery and introduce new audiences to contemporary Pakistani art.
