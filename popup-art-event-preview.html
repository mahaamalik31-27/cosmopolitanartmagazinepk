Lahore's First Pop-Up Art Event

The Weightof Softness

Fifteen emerging artists come together in an intimate exhibition exploring vulnerability, compassion, memory, resilience and the quiet strength found in softness.

Opening · 25 July 2026 · 7:00 PMExhibition · 25–30 JulyCaby & Byke Café · Lahore
Explore the Exhibition

Curatorial Theme

Strength is not
always loud.

“The Weight of Softness” creates a space where art becomes a conversation rather than simply an exhibition. Every work carries a distinct story, yet together they ask what it means to remain tender in a world that often rewards hardness.
