BECOMING /
UNBECOMING

152 emerging artists from across Pakistan come together at DOT Gulberg, Lahore.

Written by Mahaa AshrafCurated by Mahaa Malik & Maham MalikOpening: 3 October 2026On display until 3 November 2026

Lahore's contemporary art scene came alive on 3 October 2026 with the grand opening of BECOMING / UNBECOMING at DOT Gulberg, bringing together 152 emerging artists from across Pakistan in one of the city's most expansive showcases of new artistic voices.

Presented by Cosmopolitan Art Magazine × DOT and curated by Mahaa Malik and Maham Malik, the exhibition is built around ideas of identity, transformation, growth, memory, attachment, release and the tension between constructing and shedding versions of the self. Rather than asking artists to conform to one visual language, the curatorial approach allows different interpretations of becoming and unbecoming to sit beside one another, creating a layered conversation between personal experience and collective change.

For curator Mahaa Malik, the vision behind the exhibition is rooted in creating access, visibility and serious presentation opportunities for emerging artists. Her curatorial direction places established exhibition standards alongside openness to younger and less represented practices, allowing artists from different cities, educational backgrounds and stages of development to share the same platform. The aim is not simply to fill walls, but to build a space where emerging work can be seen, discussed and encountered on its own terms.

The exhibition presents a wide range of practices, including oil and acrylic painting, watercolour, gouache, drawing, mixed media, print-based work, digital art, photography, sculpture, installation and experimental contemporary pieces. Figurative and narrative works appear alongside abstraction and conceptual approaches, while works on paper, sculptural forms and material-led experiments extend the experience beyond conventional painting. Themes across the exhibition include femininity, memory, belonging, emotional states, relationships, isolation, social expectations and the shifting idea of self.

Guest of Honor

Rashid Rana

The grand opening was attended by internationally recognised contemporary artist Rashid Rana as Guest of Honor. Known for a multidisciplinary practice spanning digital work, painting, stainless-steel sculpture, video installation, photo-sculpture and photo mosaics, Rana's presence gave participating artists the opportunity to engage with one of Pakistan's most significant contemporary art figures. His participation also underscored the exhibition's focus on connecting emerging practitioners with the wider contemporary art landscape.

More than a collective display, BECOMING / UNBECOMING positions emerging practice as an active part of Pakistan's evolving cultural landscape. By bringing a large and varied group of artists into a shared curatorial framework, the exhibition highlights the breadth of experimentation taking place across the country while making room for individual stories and distinct visual languages.

BECOMING / UNBECOMING remains on display at DOT Gulberg, Lahore, until 3 November 2026, offering audiences the opportunity to experience a broad cross-section of emerging contemporary art from across Pakistan.

Curatorial selection · Becoming / Unbecoming

Highlighted Works

A closer look at distinct artistic voices in the exhibition, from Mina Yawar’s finely rendered gouache paintings to Samar Rizwan’s experiments with texture, materials and transformation. Artwork descriptions are based on the artists’ original exhibition submissions.

01 · Painting & Detail

Mina Yawar

Working in gouache on wasli, Mina Yawar brings precise, carefully constructed detail to a delicate surface. Her paintings invite viewers to linger over their intricate visual language and the intimacy of close observation.

Selected medium: gouache on wasli · Four artworks submitted

02 · Material & Transformation

Samar Rizwan

In works including Emergence, Afterglow and Ishq, Samar explores the self as something constantly changing. Fluid acrylic, sand-textured surfaces, coffee, paper and layered mixed media express the tension between attachment, release and renewal.

Selected works: Emergence (fluid acrylic on canvas, 18 × 10 in) · Afterglow (sand-textured acrylic on canvas, 18 × 18 in) · Ishq (mixed media on wooden board, 12 × 12 in)

03 · Featured Artist

Zel-e-Arsh

Featured among the exhibition's emerging artistic voices, Zel-e-Arsh is part of the wider exchange on identity and transformation at the heart of BECOMING / UNBECOMING. Further artwork details and imagery will be added once verified from the artist's portfolio.
